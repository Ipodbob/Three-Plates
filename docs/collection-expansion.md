# Broader catalogue, desserts and baking

Checked 28 September 2026. This expansion adds 32 distinct source recipes to the existing 40 linked recipes: 72 publisher recipes and 45 preserved original examples (117 total). Seven publishers are represented. The 32 additions include 15 full-yield bakes, 11 dessert choices, and nine meal-prep options; these categories overlap.

## Selection policy

The earlier 4.5/5 and 50-rating threshold describes the first 40 additions only. Following the user’s revised brief, new recipes may qualify through a useful technique, cuisine or meal-prep gap, clear ingredients and a traceable publisher. Published ratings and counts remain visible, including low-count or lower-rated selections. Editorial selection reasons are recorded per recipe; they do not masquerade as ratings or independent kitchen tests. There is no claim to identify the absolute best websites or recipes.

Facts were collected from public recipe pages and their structured metadata, then ingredient quantities and units were manually mapped. Full cooking methods, photography, comments and member-only material are not republished. Use direct publisher links for cooking. Cup and spoon conversions, approximate weights, chosen variants, omitted garnishes and assumed package sizes are disclosed. No generated recipe inherits a publisher rating.

## Coverage and remaining gaps

Search now understands dish tags, publisher, cuisine and editorial effort. Examples: traybake / tray bake, soup, pastry / pastries, savoury / savory, cake, bread, cookies, simple / beginner and complex / advanced. Effort is a Three Plates estimate based on stages and technique, not a publisher rating. Elapsed time keeps resting/chilling separate from hands-on preparation where the source makes that distinction; timing assumptions are shown.

The collection now includes one-pot dishes, traybakes, soups, stews, curries, rice bowls, pasta bakes, salads, cakes, cookies, brownies, scones, bread, focaccia, pies, quiche, puff pastry and laminated croissants. Quick fruit desserts sit alongside multi-stage projects. It is broader, not exhaustive: further work should prioritize regional African and Latin American dishes, additional seafood, gluten-free baking, chilled desserts and independently checked storage guidance rather than filling a numerical quota with duplicates. The earlier 400-recipe ambition remains a backlog, not a completed claim.

## Planning and preservation

Dessert and Baking are distinct slots alongside Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner. Dinner and dessert can share a date. Existing v3 data remains readable. Full-yield bakes default to their source yield rather than a two-person headcount; quantities up to 48 pieces can roundtrip through saved plans. Recipe details explain pieces versus people and the limits of changing tins or bake times. Pantry-only matching checks ingredients for the same full yield. Standard meals keep their previous headcount rules.

Source recipes with batch metadata can also be planned fresh, retaining one recipe ID. Original batch examples keep their existing behaviour. Rice-containing meal-prep recipes retain 24-hour fridge reminders. Freezing suitability is still unknown unless checked for the actual dish; positive reviews do not certify storage.
