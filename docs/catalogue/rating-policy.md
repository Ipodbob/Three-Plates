# Publisher rating policy

New Choose/Batch suggestions and search use publisher snapshots with at least
4/5 stars and five ratings. Counts must be whole numbers; invalid or missing
ratings are excluded. Original examples remain explicitly labelled unrated.

The catalogue retains every recipe identity and its quantities for saved plans,
batches, menus and favourites. Discovery eligibility does not delete saved data
or prevent finishing an existing meal. Ratings are snapshots, not a live feed.

This does not establish that every live publisher rating remains unchanged.
