# Rated recipe source register

Checked 28 September 2026. Eligibility: visible individual recipe-page rating ≥4.5/5 and at least 50 ratings. Publisher structured data sometimes rounded ratings to whole stars; the visible recipe-page value is used instead. Collection-page ratings were not substituted. Ratings are a snapshot, not a live feed.

All entries link to Good Food. This is a curated set, not an absolute best-rated ranking. Recipe titles and ingredient quantities are factual metadata. Cooking methods and photographs remain on the publisher site.

Each recipe stores author, publisher, source URL, source yield, rating/count/date, preparation and total times, equipment method, planning quantities and explicit assumptions. Nullable preparation time means it was not separately published. All original recipes and ingredient definitions are retained. Quantities and cooking times are not independently kitchen-tested.

The source gives some ingredients as counts, spoonfuls, handfuls or ranges. Mapping notes identify estimates, selected alternatives and omitted optional accompaniments. Counted chicken is kept separate from weight-based pantry stock. Batch freezing suitability remains unknown: a high rating or generic freezable badge is not evidence for a recipe-specific shelf life.
