## Quantity and classification review

[The quantity audit](catalogue/specialist-quantities.json) retains each source ingredient line and the conversion/omission notes. Source-provided metric quantities take precedence. US cup conversions use ingredient-specific estimates, with baking staples informed by [King Arthur's ingredient weight chart](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/ingredient-weight-chart). Produce and prepared-food cup weights are approximate planning values, not tested recipe substitutions. Source methods remain authoritative.

Counted items remain separate from weighed pantry items unless an established explicit ingredient conversion applies. Dry and cooked ingredients are kept distinct. Optional extras, garnishes, water and equipment can be omitted with notes. Explicit package net weights are multiplied; upper ends of weight ranges and estimated drained tin weights are disclosed. Recipes with unresolved main ingredients, ambiguous yields or missing usable times were held out, rather than filled with invented quantities.

Method and meal classifications start from source metadata and method indicators, with corrections for boiling pasta/grains, pancakes, uncooked pastry components and pressure cooking. Filters describe the principal equipment and may not capture every auxiliary technique. Pressure cooking always carries an allowance for pressurising/release; chilling/proving/resting indicators exclude recipes from quick-time filters. Effort labels are editorial estimates. The collection has not been independently kitchen-tested, and automated validation cannot establish recipe quality or exact ingredient equivalence.

Full cooking methods and photographs are not republished. Author, source link, rating/count, retrieval date and quantity notes appear in recipe details. Recipe-specific freezing suitability remains unverified; existing conservative storage rules are unchanged.
